Using a STM32F446RE and a digital microphone. It runs a lightweight ML model to distinguish between a "Dry Cough," "Wet Cough," and "Whooping Cough" in real-time without sending audio to the cloud (privacy-first). Acoustic Classification: Distinguish between "Normal" ambient sounds, "Dry" coughs, and "Productive/Wet" coughs. Privacy: Ensure all audio processing happens on the microcontroller; no raw audio is stored or transmitted. Low Power: Enable the device to run on a small battery for long-term respiratory monitoring.
